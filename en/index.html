About the book

What if every dream were a window into another version of your life? In this book, dreams are no longer just loose images of the night — they become portals to dimensions where we live other stories, other encounters and other possibilities.

With sensitive, poetic writing, it invites you to remember, record and interpret your dreams, discovering what they reveal about who you are, here and now.
